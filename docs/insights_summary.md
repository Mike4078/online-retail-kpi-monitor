# Insights Summary: NovaShop.cz, Jan 2025 – Jun 2026

## Headline numbers (H1 2026 vs H1 2025)
| Metric | H1 2026 | H1 2025 | Change |
|---|---|---|---|
| Net revenue | 35.9M CZK | 36.8M CZK | −2.3% |
| Orders | 10,751 | 8,829 | +21.8% |
| AOV | 3,680 CZK | 4,522 CZK | −18.6% |
| Margin % | 44.8% | 42.7% | +2.1 pp |
| Return rate | 9.3% | 7.9% | +1.4 pp |

H1 2026 revenue is understated by up to ≈0.7M CZK because app orders from 9–15 Mar 2026 are missing from the data (see "Data issues" below).

## Key insights

### 1. We sell more orders, but smaller ones
- **What:** Orders grew 22%, but revenue fell 2% because the average order value dropped by 19%.
- **Why:** Electronics, our most expensive category (~4,000 CZK per item), fell from 53% of revenue (Q1 2025) to 36% (Q2 2026). Beauty (~540 CZK per item) and Sports grew. Electronics margin % rose from ~42% to ~46% over the same period, which points to price increases. This should be confirmed with the pricing team.
- **Recommendation:** Review Electronics pricing against competitors, and test bundles and cross-sell (e.g. accessories) to lift AOV.
- **Impact:** Recovering half of the AOV drop would add about 11% to revenue at the current order volume.

### 2. Meta Prospecting is losing money since its budget was raised
- **What:** From October 2025 the Meta Prospecting campaign (CMP-M01) received about twice the budget. Its ROAS fell from ~2.4 to ~1.2 and its cost per order rose from ~1,750 to ~2,700 CZK.
- **Why:** The extra budget reached less relevant audiences. With a ~44% margin, a ROAS of 1.2 means each 1 CZK of spend returns only ~0.5 CZK of margin.
- **Recommendation:** Return the budget to its pre-October level and move the difference to Google Search Brand (ROAS ~10) and Meta Retargeting (ROAS ~6).
- **Impact:** About 0.75M CZK of spend per half-year could be redeployed (H1 2026 spend 1.75M vs ~1.0M before the increase).

### 3. NordWear returns have more than doubled
- **What:** NordWear's return rate rose from 14–18% in 2025 to 24% in Q1 2026 and 37% in Q2 2026. Other Fashion brands stayed at 13–19%.
- **Why:** The problem is limited to one brand from early 2026, which suggests a product, sizing or supplier issue.
- **Recommendation:** Raise it with the supplier, check return reasons, and pause NordWear promotions until fixed.
- **Impact:** Returns cost shipping and handling, and NordWear Denim Jeans already dropped from #5 to #9 in margin ranking.

### 4. The "Spring Mega Sale" email was broken
- **What:** The campaign had the best click-to-open rate of the year (24.7%), but only 0.4% of clickers ordered, against 10–17% for other campaigns.
- **Why:** People wanted the offer but couldn't buy. This most likely points to a broken landing page or promo code.
- **Recommendation:** Add a pre-send checklist (test the link, landing page and code on web and mobile), and an alert when orders 24 h after a send are far below normal.
- **Impact:** At a normal conversion rate, the campaign would have brought about 35 more orders.

### 5. Black Friday paid off, but the discount was expensive
- **What:** Black Friday week roughly doubled revenue (+97%) and orders (+134%) compared with the previous 4 weeks. Margin % fell from 45% to 36%, but margin in CZK was still 56% higher.
- **Recommendation:** Keep Black Friday. For 2026, test a smaller discount (e.g. 15–20%) or exclude low-margin products.

## Data issues stakeholders should know about
- **9–15 Mar 2026:** app orders are missing, so revenue for that week is understated by up to ≈0.7M CZK. The DWH team has been asked to reload the data.
- **4–6 May 2026:** GA4 recorded no web traffic. Conversion rates for those days are excluded.
- **GA4 vs backend:** GA4 reports 3–14% less revenue than the backend because of cookie consent and ad blockers. All revenue on the dashboard comes from the backend.
